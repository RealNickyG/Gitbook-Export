# Mullaley Planning

A typical Mullaley weekend is expected to atleast host most of the NU Rocketry team and have a competition level rocket launch and or certification rockets up to the L2 Category. \
Because of this, many things must be planned in advance for the day(s) to run smoothly both while onsite as well as sustaining the team while away from campus.

The planning which must be done for a rocket to be successful will not be discussed here, and is assumed to have been covered previously. This concerns itself with only the way in which the event should be ran by members of the team to be successful and reduce risk.

### Event Planning

The team should be aware, well before attendance at the event what will be launched at Mullaley by the team. This informs both the logistics behind the planning as well as the exceptions of the team while onsite.\
It should be thought through if allowing of Certifications other than critical certifications is necessary and how many are expected as well since these can cause alot of strain on the team if managed incorrectly.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you will be hosting non-critical certifications or launches at the site, it is prudent to assign a non-critical senior member to oversee the construction and preparation of each one to make sure they are compliant to NU Rocketry standards and expectations, and provide motors when deemed ready for flight to reduce stress on critical members and keep juniors engaged.
{% endhint %}

With this have these expectations be well communicated to the rest of the team well in advance so that juniors can plan accordingly, as many want to launch their own rockets and will want to know if they can / should pursue them for this launch. This will then affect attendance and engagement.

Gauge interest in attending afterwards and assign roles to accommodate the use of the launch opportunity appropriately. Essentials include:

* BBQ Operator
* Certification Advisor / Officer
* BP safety Officers
* BP box manager
* Equipment packing list checkers

Make sure these roles are known to EVERYONE and keep a public copy of job lists so they can be referenced at any point in time.

### Accommodation

Accommodation must be booked at least 4 weeks in advance to accommodate changes in the bookings (which WILL happen), and can be changed upto 2 weeks prior to check-in.\
If booking with the **Billabong Motel** payment can be made AFTER checkout but should be done ASAP.

When Booking accommodation a myriad of things will affect arrangements, such as team member preferences, the needs of the team and price. Remember many students can be easily priced out of attending an event just off of how much accommodation is, so finding a sweet-spot price while balancing the needs of the team is essential. This may mean members will have to share beds and rooms, but this MUST be very well communicated with everyone and members must have the ability to express if they are uncomfortable with arrangements so they can be properly accommodated. Clear and open communication on this is essential to ensuring the team is comfortable and well looked after so they have the platform to voice their issues.&#x20;

Rough numbers of attendees must be known to arrange rooms and confirm pricing, since each room has a different price and the number of attendees affects how much it is divided up per head. Typical pricing sits around \~$110 per person but can easily fluctuate.&#x20;

To collect attendee numbers a google form has been found to work well, HOWEVER, as always the ease this can be filled out is essential and making sure it collects and covers as much information as possible is necessary to plan this well. These forms must be easy to fill out, and if at all possible be able to be filled via mobile phone, to maximize engagement. It was found that the less accessible and easy to filled out a form is the longer it will take for a significant portion of the team to complete it, as they will put it off until the last minute. By making it accessible by mobile and easy / quick to fill out, it has been seen that more responses are given in a shorter time frame, reducing need of catch up meetings or follow ups. This means try to minimize long answer questions and try to have as much multiple choice as possible and making the form format be **Anonymous since it will now not prompt a sign-in,** just make sure the first question asked is the responders name. Absolute essentials in a Mullaley attendance form TO ASK are:

* FULL Name
* Attendance status (YES, NO, MAYBE)
* If they can be a driver for a University owned vehicle
* Time slot they plan on leaving the uni at
* Special Requirements (Food, Accom. Arrangements, preferences etc)

Essential to provide / reiterate in these forms are also:

* Dates the team will be away INCLUDING day leaving and returning to Uni
* Planned times to leave the Uni&#x20;
* Planned accommodation arrangements (Where, type of rooms / bed arrangements, etc)
* Driver forms if responder wishes to drive but has not submitted a form
* Plan for launches (Will this launch have certs and fun rockets?)
* Clothing recommendations (Depending on season warm clothes, boots, going out clothes for pub / club)
* clothing and equipment ESSENTIALS (NU Teams shirt, Cotton drill if applicable, hats, water bottles, steel caps if applicable, personal medication)
* Equipment the team plans to bring and what they can use (Remember many people who plan on launching their own rockets will need equipment such as wadding, craft knives, tape etc and EXPECT these to be at the launch site unless told otherwise. Make sure what you plan on them having access to is clearly stated to prevent issues)

There are more things which can be provided / asked but these are deemed essential, so cater your form to how the event is being planned. Just remember to try and keep accessibility as high as possible while also trying to minimize the amount of forms you must create, amount of followup with members and workload just in gathering information since actually planning the day is far more important and requires as much time as possible to get right.
