# Cars

### University Cars and Truck

With University trips, the team has access to many different types of cars from different parts of the uni. Most of course are only to carry the team to the launch site and back, but a couple are for more specific purposes that have their own requirements that MUST be fulfilled:

* Explosive Magazine Ute - the BP box MUST be in a separate cab to the passengers, so it must be in either a closed cab or tray back ute. HOWEVER if it is not in a closed cab where the cab can be locked, you cannot allow the ute to be unattended at anytime while there are explosives in the magazine and otherwise risk of the box being stolen. Keep this in mind if post-launch events are planned such as committees or staying at an accommodation as this will have to be accounted for. As of writing only the **Chem-ENGG** and **PHYS** utes are closed backs and the **Civil** utes are both open backs.
* Transporting Gazebos, tables and Pelican cases - A lot of equipment the team uses is quite large and doesn't fit well into the backs of conventional cars while accommodating passenger items such as backpacks or suit cases. This means room must be made for these items, especially for the pelicans which cannot fit into the back of a car. Typically utes or the teams truck carry these items.
* Spare Drivers -  For any long range drive (>2hrs) atleast 2 University allowed drivers should be in every car so they can swap in the case of driver fatigue, and for longer drives swap every 2 hours. Also note that the second driver for the explosives ute MUST also be BP certified as well even if the primary driver is too.
* Spare Seats - In the event of a car breaking down or otherwise being inoperable, there should be room for a cars worth of people to be able to fill empty seats in that case. Make sure this works for ANY car having this happen, as some cars may cause this to be an issue.

Remember that not only NU Rocketry or even NU Teams utilise these cars but also the university at large and some have even more volatility than others for reservations. For example the Chem-ENGG Ute can be spontaneously taken by the Geology discipline for trips and has been done even when reserved well in advance, so have backups ready, plan ahead and book as soon as you can. It is highly recommended that at the beginning of each year book essential trips such as Mullaleys and 2200 test launches and adjust the amount of cars taken to suit participants to make sure you can still attend the event. Don't forget to also coordinate these with the other teams as some may have clashing test days but do so in advance and respectfully, remember you work with the other teams just as much as your own in the end.

### Travel Approval

Prior to any trip, the University must be notified of travel. This applies to both domestic travel, to an external site as an official NU Teams / University group using university vehicles or international travel through the University.

This form is for the universities use to know where you are going, who is going, when you are going and what THEY need to book. That is, if you write what accommodation or travel arrangements you need they will book it, however if you plan on providing it yourself such as through an AirBNB or a hotel, you do not need to provide the information.

### Hire Cars

NU Teams tends to avoid booking hire cars since we have access to university vehicles and to reduce cost. However some instances will require booking hire cars, such as international travel or in order to accommodate all members. In this case a hire car will be required.

The university has a partnership with Hertz, a rental car company, so hire cars should be hired from there.

The requirements of the car should be considered when hiring, similar to selecting a university vehicle as described before, such as: will it carry BP?, how many must it seat?, what will it carry at most? etc.

Plan for the car to cost more than advertised, as other costs will come up that you may not expect.

Try to have a member or staff drive the car who is over 25, as this will greatly reduce cost, otherwise anyone over 21 is preferred but so long as a driver has a full license they are eligible.
